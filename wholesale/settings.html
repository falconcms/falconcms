Settings & Tools ​

Everything in Wholesale → Settings, then the reports, emails, text messages and scheduled tasks.

The top of the settings page: applications, coupons and promotions, never more than retail, who sees prices and the private store.

Text messages (SMS) ​

The plugin texts customers through any SMS provider that has an HTTP API, which covers almost all of them, including the Bangladeshi gateways.

SettingWhat it does
Send SMSOff, or Through my SMS provider (HTTP)
Provider URLFrom your provider's API page. Put {to} where the number goes and {message} where the text goes.
MethodGET (everything in the URL) or POST (form fields you list)
Country codePicked from a searchable list of every country. It is added to local numbers that start with 0: with Bangladesh (+880), 01711223344 becomes +8801711223344. Numbers written with + or 00 are sent as they are.
Send whenTick the events: account approved, order placed, payment received, payment due or overdue, quote ready
MessagesWhat each text says (below)

Messages. Each event has its own text. Start from a ready-made one, in English or Bangla (one click puts it in the box), and change it as you like. Placeholders such as {name}, {amount} and {balance} are filled in when it is sent, and a live count shows the characters and SMS parts. An empty box sends the built-in English text. Bangla texts fit 70 characters per SMS instead of 160, so they cost more.

Save the settings, then use Send a test SMS to check them. Staff can also text a customer by hand from the customer's screen (Send an SMS), with ready-made messages to pick from.

Sales reps ​

Add a rep with a user's email and a commission percentage; see Sales reps.

Tools ​

  • Create the login page — makes the Wholesale Login page if it is missing, brings it back from the trash, or publishes it, and sets it as the login page. Its content is never changed.
  • Rebuild the price index — the price filter and sort use what each level pays. The index updates by itself when you save a product, a category or a level, and every night. Rebuild it now if you changed retail prices in bulk (for example by import).

Reports ​

Wholesale → Reports covers a period: the last 30 or 90 days, this month, this year, or dates you choose.

Revenue, orders, average order, share of all sales, what is owed and overdue, revenue by day, top customers and products, revenue by level, and customers who have gone quiet.

  • Wholesale revenue, orders, average order and share of all sales
  • Owed on account and overdue
  • Revenue by day and by level
  • Top customers (company members counted with their company), with a CSV download
  • Top products
  • Gone quiet — customers with no order in 30, 60, 90 or 180 days
  • Sales reps — orders, revenue and commission per rep

Cancelled, failed and refunded orders are left out.

Emails ​

The plugin emails customers and staff when:

EmailTo
Application receivedYou (the address in Settings)
Application approved or rejected (with your reason)The applicant
Team invitationThe person invited to a company
Order needs approval / approved / declinedOwners and managers / the buyer
Quote readyThe customer
Payment due soon / overdueCredit customers
Reorder reminderWhoever set a reminder on a saved list
New order of a customerTheir sales rep

Every wholesale email uses one responsive layout with your shop email header image (or your logo, or your shop name), your theme's primary colour and the shop email footer text. A theme can override any of them under falcon-wholesale::emails.*.

Scheduled tasks ​

These run through the Laravel scheduler, so the site's cron must call php artisan schedule:run every minute (see Scheduled Tasks).

CommandWhenWhat it does
falcon:wholesale-reindexDaily, 03:30Rebuilds the price index
falcon:wholesale-credit-remindersDaily, 09:00Emails (and texts) payments due soon and overdue
falcon:wholesale-list-remindersDaily, 09:15Sends saved-list reorder reminders that are due
